# Glossary for store_orders.csv

## Takings
Another word for revenue: the money taken on an order, in the revenue column. Turnover, income and sales mean the same here.

## Territory
A sales territory is a region. The four territories are north, south, east and west, held in the region column. Area means the same.

## Big order
An order of more than 20 units, using the units column.

## Small order
An order of 5 units or fewer, using the units column.

## Trade
The wholesale channel. Trade customers buy through wholesale, held in the channel column.

## Web sales
Orders taken through the online channel, held in the channel column.

## Order reference
The identifier of one order. It identifies a row, so it is never listed or grouped by.
